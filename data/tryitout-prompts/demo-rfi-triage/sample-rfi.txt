Subject: Conflict between ceiling-mounted ductwork and structural beam at Level 3 corridor

Project/Area: Riverside Office Tower — Level 3, Corridor C between Grid Lines 4 and 6

Question: During coordination review, the mechanical ductwork routed along the Level 3
corridor ceiling appears to clash with a structural beam that drops lower than the
surrounding ceiling grid in this section. The duct run serves two occupied tenant
suites and is already fabricated to the dimensions shown in the current drawing set.

Additional detail: The beam depth in this bay is greater than in adjacent bays,
apparently to carry a mechanical equipment platform above. There is roughly 3 inches
of vertical clearance remaining between the bottom of the beam and the top of the
duct as currently routed, which is tighter than the coordination standard used
elsewhere on this floor. The general contractor has paused duct installation in this
bay pending a decision, but wants to keep sequencing on schedule if possible since
drywall ceiling work in the adjacent bay is scheduled to start in five business days.
No response has been received yet on a related question raised informally last week
about ceiling height in the same corridor.
