<objective>
Agent 9, Spooling & cutting: reads a text list of prefab pipe segments (spool id, nominal diameter, material, length, stock length, optional remainder) and drafts a cut list and a spool grouping for the fabricator to review. It never calculates: it copies lengths, groups by exact text equality, and marks anything that needs arithmetic or optimisation as "to compute (tool)".

Purpose: second manufacturing-stage agent through the done bar; the design deliberately keeps the weak point of the model (cutting arithmetic) out and records the missing deterministic tool as a gap.
Output: entry, SKILL.md, synthetic sample, runnable flag, live-run evidence, gaps, inventory and notice updates.
